# Contrast
Contrast is defined as something that is sharply different from something else.

By its very nature, every element in the UI has a certain amount of contrast relative to the background.

## Example

- The text on the button contrasts with the button's background

- The button's background contrasts with the card's background

- The card's background contrasts with the layout's background

## Contrast guidlines 
### Minimum AA
The text contrast ratio must be at least 4.5:1, and for large text, at least 3:1.
### Enhanced AAA 
The text contrast ratio must be at least 7:1; for large text, it must be at least 4.5:1.
